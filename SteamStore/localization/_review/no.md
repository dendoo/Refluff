# no (Norwegian Bokmål) review

Verdict: OK after fixes

## Changes
- headphones_body: `Motorene spinner opp og ned` -> `Motorene turer opp og stilner av` ("spinner opp og ned" is an English-flavoured calque that also reads as "spin up and down" (direction); "ture opp" = rev up and "stilne av" = die down say "spool up and wind down" in natural Norwegian)
- bullet_nofail: `ingen nedtelling som går ut` -> `ingen tid som renner ut` (a countdown does not "go out"; "tiden renner ut" is the Norwegian idiom for time running out)

## Checked and kept
- short_description (282 chars): natural Bokmål; "dynk teppet, spray på skum, børst det inn, skyv skittvannet vekk med nalen" keeps the verb rhythm; "Lever teppet" = the game's button; "ta fatt på det neste" idiomatic.
- Headers: Se mønsteret komme tilbake / Ekte vann, ekte gjørme / Ny jobb, nytt teppe / Få betalt, få bedre utstyr: natural, all within 28.
- Terms match the glossary: skrape, børste, nal, våtsuger, vaskemiddel, mopp, lever teppet, fartsbonus, flekkfritt, knusktørt, combo, KRAFT, AREAL, strøk (AREAL legend), du-form. Capital after a colon before a full sentence follows Språkrådet's rule.
- Bokmål, and written natively rather than as a copy of the Danish: it shares sentence skeletons with the Danish in several fields (both follow the English closely), but the words are Norwegian throughout (skittvann, nal, lønnsslipp, knusktørt, smule, skrangler, bruser, bust, fastgrodd, søl, flisgulv); no Danish forms (af, efter, gennem, bliver, øjne, ud, ind ...) leaked in.
- System requirements in the usual Norwegian Steam style (64-bit, eller nyere, eller bedre, Krever en 64-bits prosessor og et 64-bits operativsystem).

## Doubts (not changed)
- short_description "dynk teppet": "dynke" leans towards wetting / sprinkling rather than a full soak; it still reads naturally for a hose and keeps the rhythm, so it stays.
- "tørkede skorper" (short, tools_body, bullet_sim): "inntørkede" would be a touch more idiomatic for dirt, but "tørket" is correct.
