# pt-BR (Portuguese - Brazil) review

Verdict: OK after fixes

Faithful, lively Brazilian copy in the game's você register with third-person imperatives (Raspe, Entregue, Coloque),
and no European forms leaking in (no tu, enclisis-by-default, a + infinitive, sujidade, humidade, ecrã, rato):
fones, mouse, piso, marrom, placa de vídeo, borrifador, chacoalha, game over, vira lama. Glossary terms match the game
(raspador, escova, rodo, extratora, mop, entregar, POTÊNCIA / ÁREA, bônus de rapidez / impecável / bem sequinho /
combo, realce de umidade / de sujeira). Headers are natural and within 28 characters (Veja o desenho reaparecer, Água e
lama de verdade, Tapete novo a cada serviço, Fature e turbine seu kit). System-requirement lines match Brazilian Steam
wording (Requer um processador e sistema operacional de 64 bits).

Fature e turbine seu kit: good Brazilian store copy, not too slangy. Turbinar is the game's own word (the shop title is
Turbine seu equipamento), faturar is the everyday advertising verb for making money (informal but not slang, unlike
grana), and kit is common for a cleaning kit; the header is short, punchy and fits the cozy tone. Kept, as is fature in
the short description.
