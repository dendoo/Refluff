# Physical scale update — 2026-11-15

The carpet is 3.6 × 4.5 m, with 512 × 640 float simulation cells (7.03125 mm each). The active tile domain covers the entire 12 × 12 m workshop, with 1280² cells (9.375 mm each). The earlier 9 m limit has been removed: water, foam, deposits and cleaning now simulate all the way to the walls. The carpet root stays at unit scale: pile is still 20 mm high, and all tool radii/lengths/spacing/speed remain physical millimetres.

`CarpetSimulation` and `FloorConsts` are the C# sources for geometry. `Compute/SimulationDomain.hlsl` is their shared HLSL mirror, used by the three computes and all surface/debris/highlight shaders. Floor min CMM is (-4200,-3750); world origin is carpet CMM (1800,2250). ToolController checks the derived floor rectangle, including scripted input. Legacy mm-to-world offsets have been removed from the floor/foam/debris shaders. Physical foam noise periods and particle sizes/speeds are unchanged.

The render grids derive from simulation resolution: shell 128×160, water 256×320, foam 512×640; floor water 320² and floor foam 640². Floor simulation and foam vertices coincide with the four carpet edges (floor UV .35/.65 and .3125/.6875; grid subdivisions must be a multiple of 80), preserving the seamless spill join. The unchanged 48 shell layers use a 5 mm strand pitch, 12 mm clump pitch and 24 mm maximum bend. The builder writes their UV-dependent material values, a collider 3.6×.02×4.5 and SimSettings.DX 7.03125. Runtime `_DX` also derives from physical size to protect against old assets.

Narrow blades integrate a 3×3 subcell quadrature; wide tools retain their cheap point query. The tool's physical footprint is unchanged. Across 19 angles × 16 fractional cell phases, scraper integrated area varies at most 0.631% on carpet and 0.942% on floor. EdgePull integrates an outward density with support at least two floor cells (60/40 for two full bands); along-edge overlap and half-shares at corners conserve every source texel. Emission rates and per-cell caps scale with cell area relative to the original 3.125 mm grid (5.0625×), and spawn positions jitter within the new cell, retaining physical event density.

Handover travel is 8.5 m out / 8.3 m in, sufficient to move the enlarged carpet beyond the room wall. The player/camera and logical carpet frame still remain stationary during handover.

Carpet cell count remains 77.8% above the original 384×480. The full-room floor has 77.8% more cells than the previous 960² / 9 m domain, or 2.78× the original 768². The additional floor RT/inject-buffer allocation is approximately 94.6 MB relative to the 9 m domain. The physical timestep and velocity limits are unchanged. Maximum nominal Courant numbers are 0.393 carpet and 0.419 floor.

Histogram sums use 512 fixed-point units per amount (both C# and HLSL), keeping cap 16. The worst case 327680×16×512=2684354560 fits uint32; the former 1024 scale overflowed at the new resolution. The GPU test fills every sample above cap 16 and verifies the exact accumulated value and cell count.

Verification scripts: `verify_scale_math.py` checks domain alignment, all 2300 edge texels' conservation, coverage phase variation, emission density and CFL. `verify_scale_gpu.cs` checks actual runtime dimensions/materials, hose physical centroid, and direct GPU water/slurry/foam/dirt conservation. Existing `verify_controls.cs`, `verify_live.cs`, `verify_cycle.cs` use the enlarged room/table positions. The live scripts preserve save bytes and disable achievement side effects where needed.

## Full-room floor extension

FloorConsts.FloorSizeMm = 12000 and FloorW = 1280 mirror SimulationDomain.hlsl. All three computes use this shared domain; no new shader keywords or variants were introduced. Rendering meshes cover the same 12 m width. CarpetSceneBuilder must set floor water subdivisions to FloorW / 4 and floor foam to FloorW / 2, then rebuild the scene. The 9.375 mm simulation cell and 18.75 mm foam vertex spacing are retained, so the edge-transfer distribution and physical tool coverage do not change.

Floor statistics use fixed-point scale 128 in both K_FReduce and FloorSimulation. A uniform 12 mm layer across 1,638,400 cells gives 2,516,582,400, safely below uint32; the previous scale 256 would overflow. This changes readback quantization only, not simulation state or tool strength.
