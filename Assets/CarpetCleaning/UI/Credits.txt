# Carpet designs
The five rugs are photographs of historic carpets from museum open-access collections (public domain, CC0).
The Metropolitan Museum of Art: Carpet, Turkey (1974.149.29); 'Transylvanian' Carpet, Turkey (22.100.91); Carpet, European (10.152.14); Salor Main Carpet, Turkmenistan (1974.149.46).
The Art Institute of Chicago: Carpet ("Ryijy" / "Rya"), Central Finland (1924.458).
