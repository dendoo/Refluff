## Changes
- tools_body: `plöj sedan bort sörjan` -> `ploga sedan bort sörjan` (in Swedish a snowplough "plogar"; "plöja" is ploughing a field, so "plöj bort" misses the snow-plough image the English is going for; "ploga bort" is the everyday expression, as in "ploga bort snön")

- Terms match the glossary: skrapa, borste, gummiskrapa, våtsug, golvrengöring, mopp, lämna över, fartbonus, fläckfri, snustorr, combo, KRAFT, YTA, svep (YTA legend), du-form. Lower case after a colon is the normal Swedish style and is used consistently.

- System requirements in Steam's Swedish wording (64-bitars, eller senare, eller bättre, Kräver en 64-bitars processor och ett 64-bitars operativsystem).
