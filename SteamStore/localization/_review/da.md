# da (Danish) review

Verdict: OK after fixes

## Changes
- headphones_body: `Alle maskiner er optagelser af rigtige maskiner.` -> `Alle maskinlyde er optagelser af rigtige maskiner.` (in Danish "all machines are recordings of real machines" reads literally odd; it is the machine sounds that are recordings)
- bullet_nofail: `intet ur, der løber ud` -> `ingen tid, der løber ud` (a clock does not run out in Danish; time does: "tiden løber ud" is the idiom)

## Checked and kept

- Headers: Se mønstret dukke frem igen / Ægte vand, ægte mudder / Nyt job, nyt tæppe / Tjen penge, opgrader grejet: natural, all within 28.
- Terms match the glossary: skraber, børste, svaber, vådstøvsuger, gulvvask, moppe, aflever, fartbonus, pletfrit, knastørt, combo, KRAFT, AREAL, grej (shop title "Giv dit grej et løft"), strøg (AREAL legend), du-form. Capital after a colon before a full sentence follows the Danish rule.
- Written natively, not a copy of the Norwegian: the Danish and Norwegian texts share sentence skeletons in several fields (tools_body, water_body, rugs_body, alt texts), which is expected for two languages this close translating the same English, but vocabulary and idiom are each the language's own (sjask / snavsevand, svaber, grej, knastørt, lønseddel, krumme, rasler, syder vs the Norwegian skittvann, nal, utstyr, knusktørt, lønnsslipp, smule, skrangler, bruser). No Norwegian word or spelling leaked in (checked av/etter/gjennom/blir/øyne/ut/inn and similar: none).
- System requirements in Steam's Danish style (Kræver en 64-bit processor og styresystem, eller nyere, eller bedre).
