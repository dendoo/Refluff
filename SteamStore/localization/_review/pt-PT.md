# pt-PT (Portuguese - Portugal) review

Verdict: OK as written

Faithful, natural European Portuguese in the game's tu register, with consistent Portugal-only grammar and vocabulary
and no Brazilian forms leaking in: enclisis (cobre-as, entranha-se, Entrega-o), personal infinitive (até deixares),
a + infinitive (Vê o padrão a reaparecer, chega logo a voar), mal = as soon as, a sério, auscultadores, rato, chão,
castanha, cor-de-rosa, nódoas, sujidade, pó, placa gráfica, sistema operativo, contagem decrescente, aterra, passa ao
seguinte. Glossary terms match the game (raspador, escova, puxa-água, aspirador húmido, esfregona, entregar,
melhoramentos, POTÊNCIA / ÁREA, bónus de rapidez / impecável / sequíssimo / combo, realce de humidade / de sujidade).
Headers are natural and within 28 characters (Vê o padrão a reaparecer, Água a sério, lama a sério, Novo trabalho, novo
tapete, Recebe a paga, afina o kit). reveal_body already avoids the one-pass-at-a-time calque (passagem a passagem. Dá
gosto ver.). System-requirement lines match Portuguese Steam wording.

Puxa-água: the everyday Portuguese word for a floor squeegee (also sold as puxa-águas); in running text (com o
puxa-água, um puxa-água empurra a água suja) it reads naturally, and the masculine article is right.

None.

## Doubts (left as written)
- hdr_paid / short_description: recebe a paga is colloquial and slightly old-fashioned, but it is genuine European
  Portuguese, warm and short enough for the header; receber o pagamento would be more neutral but does not fit
  better. Kept.
- short_description: empurra a lama com o puxa-água puts push next to the lexicalised pull-water; no reader takes the
  compound literally, so it was kept.
- headphones_body: a espuma crepita (crackles) for foam fizzes is acceptable for popping bubbles; kept.
